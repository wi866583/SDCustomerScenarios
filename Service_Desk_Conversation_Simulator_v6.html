How it works

  • You are the IT Service Desk Technician. A customer is contacting you with a technical issue.
  • At each turn, choose the response you would actually give. There is no “undo” button, just like on a real call.
  • Watch the customer's reactions and the Customer Confidence meter. Your choices shape how the conversation goes.
  • At the end, a debrief scores your communication and shows the most effective responses.
